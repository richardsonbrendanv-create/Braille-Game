Notes

  • SSS: use Law of Cosines to get an angle, then Law of Sines.
  • SAS: Law of Cosines for the opposite side.
  • ASA/AAS: find the third angle, then Law of Sines.
  • SSA: ambiguous (0, 1, or 2 triangles) — app shows all valid ones.

How to use this app

  • Unit Circle Trainer: practice exact values; use keyboard 1–4 and Enter.
  • Quick Tools: fast deg↔rad, inverse trig, and exact special-angle values.
  • Graphing Lab: plot y = A·f(B(x − C)) + D for sin, cos, or tan; shows period and key info.
  • Triangle Solver: enter any 3 values (≥1 side); supports SSS, SAS, ASA/AAS, and SSA ambiguity.
  • Identity Drill: T/F questions from common identities + traps.
